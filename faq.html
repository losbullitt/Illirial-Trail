Cantebury Trails — player guide (v8.4.0)

Lead a caravan from Cantebury west to the harbor of New Isil. Manage supplies, fight monsters, recruit companions, and survive the road.

Getting started

  1. Open the game in a modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge).
  2. Choose Create new character or Use preset leader.
  3. In Cantebury, stock supplies at the City shop, check Party gear, then open Depart.
  4. Pick your first westbound fork and press on, day by day.

Public play link: https://losbullitt.github.io/Illirial-Trail/index.html

Core loop

  • Prepare in town — buy supplies, heal, recruit, equip gear.
  • March — each travel day spends supplies and may trigger encounters or ruins.
  • Fight — pick actions for each party member, then end the round.
  • Settle — rest at inns/stables, take tavern quests, visit keeps, then depart again.
  • Win — reach New Isil after enough journey days (see FAQ).

The trail

The road forks. On the way west you pick one town from each fork pair (you do not visit both on the same outbound march). 

Cantebury
    ├─ Gustaf → Grosjean     ─┐  lower fork — pick one path
    └─ Idlwyld → Brookside   ─┘
              ↓
    ├─ Hollow Banks          ─┐  upper fork — pick one
    └─ Glennhardt → Arpery   ─┘
              ↓
           Solem
              ↓
         New Isil (harbor)
  • Each leg is a few travel days (rolled once per road and kept for that campaign).
  • Eastbound, you usually reverse the path you took, with options to revisit towns.
  • Adventure tabs let you trek near a town without committing to a full leg west.

Combat & party

  • Fighting line: up to 5 companions.
  • Classes: Soldier, Priest, Mercenary, Mage (and later Paladin via an optional quest).
  • Stats: Strength, Intelligence, Stamina, Luck — they drive damage, spells, HP growth, and crits.
  • Fallen companions can be revived at chapels (gold) or with a Potion of Life (often half HP).
  • Keep supplies topped up — running out on the road is deadly.

Saves

  • Use the on-screen Save / Load controls (campaign slots in the browser).
  • Saves stay on this device / this browser — there is no cloud account.
  • Clearing site data or switching browsers will not carry your run with you.

FAQ

Do I need to install anything?

No. The public link runs in the browser. Local play only needs a simple file server if you are developing offline.

How do I win?

Reach New Isil after enough journey days (around 300 by default; the game can extend this for the final boss march). After journey day ~270, SK Kew Kumber can block the last westward road until defeated.

I don’t know what to do in Cantebury.

Short checklist: City shop → buy supplies / potions → Party tab → equip weapons → Adventure (optional practice) → Depart → choose a fork.

What are supplies for?

Each travel / adventure / quest day consumes supplies. No food means you cannot safely press on.

What are gems for?

Loot from fights and ruins. Sell them at shops for gold.

Can I visit both forks on one trip west?

No outbound. You may see the other road later on a return or detour if that town is available.

What is “Illirial Trail” vs “Cantebury Trails”?

Cantebury Trails is the game title. Illirial-Trail is the GitHub project name. Same game.

Is there multiplayer?

No. Single-player only.
